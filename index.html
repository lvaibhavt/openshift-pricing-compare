OpenShift Pricing Compare

Red Hat OpenShift on OCI (BYOM) vs managed OpenShift on AWS (ROSA), Azure (ARO), Google Cloud (OSD) and IBM Cloud (ROKS). Indicative on‑demand list prices; verify with each provider.

1Common requirements

What the OpenShift cluster needs, independent of cloud. Sizes are physical cores (1 core = 1 OCI OCPU = 2 vCPU elsewhere). Storage performance is a per‑volume target that every cloud is sized to meet.

2Cloud‑specific choices

Region, shape per node role, and storage type + performance settings for each cloud. Defaults are the cheapest option that meets Step 1; anything that falls short is marked ⚠. Only regions where the OpenShift offering is available are listed.

3Pricing

Rate card: OpenShift fees and subscriptions (editable)

Assumptions and sources

  • OCI: BYOM. OCI compute + Block Volume + your own Red Hat subscription (workers only; masters and infra are exempt). OCI prices are identical in every region. Regions: all OC1 commercial regions (OCI regions documentation).
  • AWS ROSA: regions per AWS ROSA endpoints page. HCP isn't offered in us‑west‑1. HCP = hosted control plane at $0.25/cluster‑hr; Classic = masters on your EC2 + $0.03/cluster‑hr. Workers pay EC2 + ROSA fee per 4 vCPU.
  • Azure ARO: shapes per ARO support policy (masters must be ≥ 8 vCPU D/E‑series). Regions per Azure products‑by‑region; confirm with az provider show -n Microsoft.RedHatOpenShift. Workers pay VM + ARO fee. No bare metal.
  • Google OSD: regions per OpenShift Dedicated service definition. Masters and infra on your GCE VMs; workers pay VM + OSD fee. No bare metal.
  • IBM ROKS: VPC regions per IBM Cloud docs. Masters managed at no charge; infra and workers pay VM + OCP license. Bare metal on Classic.
  • Non‑US regional prices use approximate uplifts over the cheapest US region. Storage IOPS/throughput limits are per volume. Excludes egress, load balancers, snapshots, support and reserved/committed discounts.
