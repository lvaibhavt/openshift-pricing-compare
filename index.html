OpenShift Pricing Compare

Red Hat OpenShift on OCI (BYOM) vs managed OpenShift on AWS (ROSA), Azure (ARO), Google Cloud (OSD) and IBM Cloud (ROKS). Indicative on‑demand list prices; verify with each provider.

3Pricing

Rate card: fees, subscriptions and storage (editable)

Assumptions

  • OCI: BYOM. OCI compute + Block Volume + your own Red Hat OpenShift subscription (workers only; masters and infra nodes are exempt). OCI prices are identical in every region.
  • AWS ROSA: HCP = control plane hosted, $0.25/cluster‑hr, no master EC2. Classic = masters on your EC2, $0.03/cluster‑hr. Workers pay EC2 + ROSA fee per 4 vCPU. Infra pays EC2 only.
  • Azure ARO: masters and infra run as your VMs (no OpenShift fee on them); workers pay VM + ARO fee. No bare metal.
  • Google OSD (CCS): masters and infra on your GCE VMs; workers pay VM + OSD fee. No bare metal.
  • IBM ROKS: masters managed by IBM at no charge; infra and workers pay VM + OCP license. Bare metal on Classic.
  • Regional prices use approximate uplifts over the cheapest US region. Excludes egress, load balancers, support and reserved/committed discounts.
